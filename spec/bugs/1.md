# Bug results

Implemented all seven fixes:

- Chronicle scrolls to the bottom whenever new entries arrive, even after manually scrolling up.
- Added a localized **Round info** button in the toolbar and popup headers. It preserves the draft order, possible earlier choices, and options offered to you for the current round. Refresh/reconnect recovery uses only your own private draft memory.
- Human seats are never replaced by AI on disconnect, host resume, or rematch. Removed the human turn timeout and its lobby setting so the game waits indefinitely. Deliberately added AI seats remain AI.
- Chronicle entries have clear separators at each character call. Events involving you, including transfers and attacks against your characters, use a teal highlight in both the sidebar and full chronicle.
- Added a visible animated hourglass beside the acting player, including drafting and interrupts, and in your own resource panel when you act.
- Blackmailer cannot select itself. The shared target filter enforces this for the UI, AI, and engine action validation.
- Scholar cards select/highlight on click and require **Confirm** to keep a card. Repeated card clicks do not submit the choice.
